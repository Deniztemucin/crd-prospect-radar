#AccountRegionAUM $bnOwnerScoreWhy now

Every signal, newest first. Points show what each one currently adds to its account's score after time decay and job-ad checks.

Signal weights

Points a brand-new, fully trusted signal adds. Each type is capped at 1.5× its weight so ten job ads can't outweigh one CIO change.

Fit, decay and ghost-job rules

AUM carries the most fit weight. Job ads open longer than the stale limit, reposted ads, and ads with no other signal at the same firm in 90 days lose confidence and are flagged for a human check.

Test a job title

Handles accents, gendered forms such as Leiterin and (m/w/d), and VP / vice president.

Technology watch list

One per line: name | category | words to look for in job ads.

Personas and title variants

Starter list in English plus seven languages: have native speakers check it before relying on it. One line per variant as language: title. Contacts are people to reach; "hiring only" roles are ones you track but don't contact.

How this goes live without touching Salesforce

1 · Salesforce reportScheduled weekly report of EMEA CRD accounts, emailed with the CSV attached.
2 · Power AutomateSaves attachments only from that sender and subject to SharePoint /ProspectRadar/inbox with a dated name.
3 · Signals fileCopilot research pass or analyst adds dated, sourced signals in the template below.
4 · This radarChecks columns, rejects wrong files, scores, ranks, drafts the Copilot prompt.
5 · Link in SalesforceA URL button on the account page opens the radar at that account (…#acct-a1).

Today steps 1 to 3 are manual: drop the two latest files below. Wiring the radar to read SharePoint directly, and sharing owner changes across the team, is the part that needs a developer.
